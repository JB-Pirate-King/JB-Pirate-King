### AIS 메세지 변환 구조
    VHF AIS 신호 (초단파)
    ⇒AIS Binary Message (ITU-R M.1371,)
    ⇒ NMEA sentence (!AIVDM)
    !AIVDM,1,1,,A,177KQJ5000G?tO`K>RA1wUbN0TKH,0*5C
    !AIVDM,<fragment count>,<fragment number>,<msg id>,<channel>,<payload>,<fill bits>*<checksum>
    → Decoder (AIS parser)
    ⇒ JSON / CSV 데이터셋
